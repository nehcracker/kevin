# Mineral Trade Finance

## International Financing Advisory for Mineral and Commodity Transactions

Kevin Graham provides independent financial advisory and
capital-sourcing support for eligible mineral and commodity trading
transactions. His work focuses on assessing financing requirements,
reviewing transaction structures and connecting qualified businesses
with potential banks, private credit providers, institutional financiers
and investment partners.

Mineral transactions often require significant working capital to secure
supply, meet purchase commitments, arrange transportation and complete
delivery. Kevin works with businesses seeking financing for commercially
supported transactions involving gold, copper, cobalt, lithium,
manganese and other traded minerals.

Support is available for qualifying transactions across Africa, Europe,
the Middle East and other international markets. Each opportunity is
assessed on its own commercial merits, documentation, counterparties,
jurisdiction and financing requirements.

## Mineral Trade Finance Advisory Services

### Mineral Purchase Financing

Advisory and capital-sourcing support for established mineral traders
and qualified commodity buyers seeking funds to purchase minerals from
verified suppliers. Initial assessment considers the commodity,
quantity, price, supplier credentials, buyer arrangements and proposed
repayment source.

### Commodity Trade Finance

Transaction assessment and financing structuring support for domestic
and cross-border commodity trades. Relevant considerations include
purchase and sale contracts, payment terms, delivery obligations,
logistics, insurance and counterparty risk.

### Pre-Export Finance

Financing advisory for eligible producers and exporters seeking capital
to prepare and deliver minerals against documented export or supply
commitments. Assessment may cover production capacity, stock
availability, permits, export requirements and confirmed buyer demand.

### Working Capital Finance

Capital-sourcing support for established mineral businesses seeking
liquidity for procurement, supplier payments, storage, transport,
handling and other trade-related costs.

### Offtake-Backed Financing

Assessment of transactions supported by credible offtake agreements,
purchase commitments or established buyer relationships. The strength
and enforceability of the underlying commercial arrangements form part
of the review.

### Trade Finance Instruments

Advisory support for transactions involving recognised trade finance
instruments, including documentary letters of credit and bank
guarantees. Instrument availability, issuance and acceptance depend on
the participating banks, transaction structure, compliance review and
formal approvals.

## Transaction Assessment and Financing Process

### 1. Initial Review

The proposed transaction is reviewed to understand the commodity,
parties, jurisdictions, transaction value, funding requirement and
intended use of funds.

### 2. Documentation Review

Available corporate, supplier, buyer, product, regulatory and
contractual documents are examined. Further information is requested
where material gaps affect the assessment.

### 3. Transaction Structuring

Potential financing structures are considered in light of the
procurement cycle, payment terms, delivery schedule, security options,
repayment source and risk allocation.

### 4. Capital Sourcing

Where the transaction meets the relevant screening requirements,
suitable potential financing partners may be identified and approached,
subject to mandate, confidentiality and applicable compliance
procedures.

### 5. Due Diligence and Negotiation

Interested financiers conduct their own due diligence and determine
whether to proceed. Commercial terms, conditions, security requirements
and documentation are negotiated directly with the relevant parties.

### 6. Financing Completion

Financing proceeds only after the relevant financier has completed its
reviews, issued the required approvals and executed definitive
agreements. Disbursement follows the agreed contractual and banking
procedures.

## Information Required for an Initial Assessment

To support an initial review, prepare the following information where
available:

-   Company profile and incorporation details.
-   Details of directors, beneficial owners and authorised
    representatives.
-   Mineral type, grade, form and quantity.
-   Supplier identity and evidence of supply authority.
-   Evidence of mineral origin, ownership and applicable licences.
-   Buyer details and purchase or offtake agreement.
-   Purchase price, sale price and transaction cost breakdown.
-   Delivery terms, destination, logistics and insurance arrangements.
-   Required financing amount and proposed use of funds.
-   Proposed repayment source and payment schedule.
-   Relevant export, import and regulatory documents.

The exact requirements depend on the commodity, countries involved,
transaction structure and prospective financier.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is mineral trade finance?

Mineral trade finance refers to financing arrangements that support the
purchase, sale, movement, processing or export of minerals. Depending on
the transaction, financing may address procurement costs, working
capital, pre-export expenses or payment obligations under a trade
contract.

### Do you arrange financing for gold purchases?

Kevin assesses gold purchase transactions supported by credible
suppliers, verifiable product specifications, evidence of origin and
ownership, appropriate regulatory documents and a clear buyer or
repayment arrangement. Any financing opportunity depends on the
transaction review and the requirements of prospective financiers.

### Which minerals are eligible for assessment?

Transactions involving gold, copper, cobalt, lithium, manganese,
tantalum, tin, tungsten, graphite, iron ore and other commercially
traded minerals are considered. Eligibility depends on product
specifications, legal status, counterparties, marketability and
transaction economics.

### Do you work with mineral businesses in Africa?

Yes. Kevin considers qualifying mineral and commodity transactions
involving African markets and cross-border trade with counterparties in
Europe, the Middle East and other regions. Each transaction must meet
applicable legal, regulatory and compliance requirements.

### What documents are required?

Initial review usually requires a company profile, supplier and buyer
details, mineral specifications, evidence of origin and ownership,
relevant licences, purchase or offtake contracts, a transaction cost
breakdown and details of the financing requirement. Additional documents
may be requested during due diligence.

### Do you provide loans directly?

Kevin provides independent financial advisory and capital-sourcing
support. He is not a direct lender. Where appropriate, he seeks to
connect qualified transactions with potential financing partners. All
financing decisions rest with the relevant financier and remain subject
to due diligence, credit approval and definitive agreements.

### Is financing guaranteed after an initial assessment?

No. An initial assessment does not constitute a financing commitment or
approval. A financier must complete its own due diligence, risk
assessment and approval process before offering terms or committing
funds.

### What transaction size do you consider?

Transaction size is assessed in context. Relevant factors include the
commodity, jurisdiction, supplier and buyer credentials, transaction
economics, documentation quality and the requirements of potential
financing partners.
